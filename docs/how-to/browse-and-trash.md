# Find items and move them to the Trash

Use this page to find items in a saved scan, check the ones you want and move exactly those to the Trash after a review. Disk Cleaner never deletes permanently and never acts on its own.

## Browse a scan

You need a scan first; see the [first scan tutorial](../tutorials/first-scan.md).

1. Open **Browse files**. Click a folder row to go inside. Use the back chevron or a breadcrumb to return; the row you came from stays in view. Browsing uses the saved scan and does not rescan.
2. Search filters the entries; sort by Size, Name or Modified. Select a row to see its path, date and coverage in the inspector.

## Move items to the Trash

1. Tick the checkbox on each item you want. Selecting a row to read it is separate from checking it. The bar shows "N checked for Trash" and their size.
2. Press Review for Trash. The page lists the exact paths. Search, tabs and navigation are locked while it is open.
3. Press Cancel or the back chevron to leave with nothing moved, back at the same folder, position and selection.
4. Press Move to Trash to go ahead. This is the only button that moves anything.
5. Read the outcome per item: **moved**, **not moved** with the reason, or **unknown** with the last known Trash location. Rescan to refresh the numbers.

Moving to the Trash does not free space until you empty it. See [Limits and your data](../explanation/limits.md) for what is not promised.
